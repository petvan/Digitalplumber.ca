    About this paper

    Digital Plumber is an independent daily briefing for network and IT operations practitioners: senior network engineers, NetDevOps leads, AIOps architects, and the teams keeping infrastructure running.

    Each morning an AI editor searches the web, picks the most substantive developments across AI ops, network automation, agentic AI, security and infrastructure, and writes them up for practitioners, not marketers. It's AI-curated and AI-summarized, so verify before acting on anything here.
