The draft restructures guidance around Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 and expands advice on risk management, asset management, monitoring and detection, system-management security, and zero-trust principles across sectors including water, transportation, and IIoT. Public comment period runs through November 30, 2026.

NISTOT SecurityZero TrustCybersecurity Framework 2.0

NIST's SP 800-82r4 represents the first major OT security guidance update to align with Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, restructuring the document around modern risk categories and expanding coverage of zero-trust principles for operational technology environments. The revision directly addresses the convergence of IT and OT security in critical infrastructure, with explicit guidance on segmentation, asset inventory, continuous monitoring, and detection strategies tailored to systems where availability, safety, and real-time constraints differ fundamentally from traditional IT. For network operations teams supporting water utilities, transportation systems, and industrial IoT, this provides standards-backed validation for zero-trust micro-segmentation, automated vulnerability scanning adapted for OT constraints, and behavioral anomaly detection.
