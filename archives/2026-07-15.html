Astera LabsScorpio X-SeriesGPU FabricIn-Network ComputeAI Infrastructure

Astera Labs' Scorpio X-Series 320 Lane represents a strategic shift from passive connectivity to active compute participation in data center fabric switching. The switch embeds two key hardware acceleration engines: Hypercast handles AllGather, AllScatter, and All-to-All collective operations by moving data replication into the switch itself, while In-Network Compute offloads AllReduce and ReduceScatter operations directly to switch silicon rather than consuming GPU cycles. For network operations practitioners, this represents a practical shift in how infrastructure teams approach scale-up clustering: rather than relying on proprietary interconnects (NVLink, Infinity Fabric), memory-semantic open standards enable multi-vendor GPU strategies. The switch's support for NVLink Fusion, UALink, and standard Ethernet-based protocols reduces vendor lock-in while addressing the reality that training clusters at 100K+ GPU scale now face interconnect, not compute, as the binding constraint on utilization. Production is ramping H2 2026, with early deployments already underway at major hyperscalers.
