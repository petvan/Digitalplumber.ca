    Strike on Yandex data centre triggers Russian BGP collapse

    Arista scales Ethernet up to 144 accelerators per domain

    Why it matters Ethernet scale-up to 144 accelerators via ESUN gives operators a credible alternative to proprietary accelerator interconnects.

    Arista Networks · Oct 10, 2026 edition
