  3. Routing·Analysis

    BGP maximum-prefix limits are disconnecting healthy networks

    Internet Society research shows maximum-prefix limits on BGP sessions, designed to prevent route leaks, inadvertently disconnect networks when they legitimately grow their route announcements.

    Why it matters Evidence that BGP max-prefix limits cause the outages they were meant to prevent should prompt immediate review of peering configs.

    Internet Society · Nov 11, 2026 edition

  4. DC networking·Industry news

    Arista splits AI networking between ESUN and Ultra Ethernet

    Arista announced dual Ethernet standards for scale-up and scale-out: ESUN for scale-up (supporting up to 144 accelerators per rack, 1,024 cross-rack) and UEC-based Ethernet for scale-out.

    Why it matters Arista's ESUN scale-up and UEC scale-out split defines the Ethernet design choices for AI clusters over the next two years.

    Fierce Network / NextPlatform · Nov 12, 2026 edition

  5. AIOps·Primary source

    Exchange Online traffic fell 85 percent during Microsoft outage

    Kentik's Director of Internet Analysis published real-time traffic analytics showing Exchange Online traffic fell 85% below expected levels at 17:35 UTC on November 12, providing independent quantification of outage severity.

    Why it matters Independent traffic data quantifying the Exchange Online outage shows how external telemetry beats vendor status pages during incidents.

    Kentik Internet Analysis · Nov 13, 2026 edition
