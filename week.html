5 developments that mattered

Picked by the AI editor

  1. Automation·Vendor release

    NetBox opens its source of truth to AI agents

    Arista CloudVision, AWS VPC IPAM, and Cisco Nexus Dashboard integrations become generally available in October; NetBox Agents gains pre-built agents for change request review, lifecycle compliance, and data import in public preview; the NetBox Platform MCP Server (now GA) enables AI agents to query and change infrastructure in plain language.

    Why it matters Source of truth becomes agent-callable: NetBox MCP server GA plus Arista, AWS IPAM and Nexus Dashboard integrations change day-to-day automation design.

    NetBox Labs / Globe Newswire · Oct 15, 2026 edition

  2. DC networking·Industry news

    Ultra Ethernet 1.1 adds programmable congestion management

    UEC released Version 1.1 on October 13 to boost scale-up networking and improve congestion control as AI workloads surge through high-end networks.

    Why it matters UEC 1.1's programmable congestion management directly affects how AI fabrics are tuned and which NICs and switches operators qualify next.

    SDxCentral · Oct 15, 2026 edition

  3. Routing·Industry news

    RPKI now protects 70 percent of internet routes

    RPKI-valid routing jumped from 54% to 70.3% in a year, driven largely by China's adoption. Nearly half of all invalid routes reach only a single vantage point, and when wrong networks announce addresses, 71% reach just one peer.

    Why it matters RPKI validity crossing 70% shifts route-origin hygiene from aspiration to baseline expectation for peering and filtering policy.

    IPinfo · Oct 17, 2026 edition

  4. AIOps·Vendor release

    Selector lets network teams build and version their own agents

    Selector introduced Selector Foundry, a development and runtime environment that lets network operations teams build, test, version and govern their own AI agents inside the Selector platform, where operators author agents where their telemetry is correlated, store them in Git, and validate against historical incidents before production.

    Why it matters Git-versioned, testable NetOps agents validated against historical incidents is the DevNetOps pattern practitioners have been asking for.

    Selector · Oct 15, 2026 edition

  5. Security·Industry news

    Ivanti Connect Secure zero-day chain gives attackers root

    CISA urged immediate patching of CVE-2026-104935, an Ivanti Connect Secure authentication bypass exploited in zero-day attacks since October 1st that lets remote threat actors execute arbitrary commands without credentials.

    Why it matters Unauthenticated root RCE chain actively exploited on Ivanti Connect Secure since October 1 demands immediate edge patching and compromise hunting.

    BleepingComputer · Oct 14, 2026 edition
