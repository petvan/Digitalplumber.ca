    Selector lets network teams build and version their own agents

    Selector introduced Selector Foundry, a development and runtime environment that lets network operations teams build, test, version and govern their own AI agents inside the Selector platform, where operators author agents where their telemetry is correlated, store them in Git, and validate against historical incidents before production.

    Why it matters Operators can build, Git-version and test their own NetOps agents against historical incidents, bringing CI/CD discipline to AIOps.

    Selector · Oct 15, 2026 edition

    UEC released Version 1.1 on October 13 to boost scale-up networking and improve congestion control as AI workloads surge through high-end networks.

    Why it matters UEC 1.1 adds programmable congestion management, firming up Ethernet as a credible InfiniBand alternative for million-GPU fabrics.
