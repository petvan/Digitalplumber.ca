  1. Routing·Industry news

    Juniper router feature triggered nationwide FAA ground stop

    During routine FAA telecommunications maintenance on November 13, a newly installed Juniper router generated over 1,000 connection attempts every 4-5 seconds, cascading through the network and affecting 17 of 22 en-route air traffic control centers.

    Why it matters A single Juniper router feature cascaded into a nationwide FAA ground stop, a concrete lesson in change control and blast-radius testing.

    Tom's Hardware, Transportation.gov, Cellstream · Nov 15, 2026 edition

  2. Routing·Analysis

    Cloudflare resolver hijack propagated despite valid RPKI ROA

    On November 13–14 UTC, a route leak/hijack of Cloudflare's 1.1.1.0/24 DNS resolver prefix degraded Canadian resolver reachability.

    Why it matters Shows RPKI ROAs alone don't stop propagation when transit networks accept invalids, directly affecting operators' routing security posture.

    LogicMonitor · Nov 17, 2026 edition

  3. Startups·Industry news

    Traversal raises $120M for autonomous AI site reliability engineers

    Traversal, which builds autonomous AI site reliability engineers to diagnose and resolve software outages, raised $120 million in Series B funding from Kleiner Perkins, Redpoint Ventures, Sequoia Capital, Battery Ventures, and Khosla Ventures at a $1 billion valuation.

    Why it matters $120M at $1B valuation for autonomous AI SREs signals real money behind agentic incident response that SRE leads will be asked about.

    Parsers Substack / Tech Funding Digest · Nov 16, 2026 edition

  4. Routing·Analysis

    Traffic rebalancing amplified Microsoft 365 outage into deadlock

    Technical analysis reveals Microsoft 365 outage was amplified by cascading routing failures. Initial health-check failures triggered traffic rebalancing, which itself generated a surge of outbound routing calls that further overloaded backend servers.

    Why it matters Health-check-triggered rebalancing created a self-amplifying deadlock, a failure mode every SRE should design load balancing against.

    System Design Academy, Enow Software, Azure Blog UK · Nov 15, 2026 edition

  5. Automation·Industry news

    Unimus to add native MCP support for agents

    Unimus, a network configuration management platform, is adding native Model Context Protocol (MCP) support in an upcoming release, enabling AI agents to govern network changes. This briefing was conducted ahead of AutoCon 6.

    Why it matters Native MCP in a config management tool means agents can govern network changes, a practical DevNetOps milestone briefed at AutoCon 6.

    Packet Pushers · Nov 14, 2026 edition
