Picked by the AI editor

  1. Routing·Industry news

    Juniper router feature triggered FAA nationwide ground stop

    During routine FAA telecommunications maintenance on November 13, a newly installed Juniper router generated over 1,000 connection attempts every 4-5 seconds, cascading through the network and affecting 17 of 22 en-route air traffic control centers.

    Why it matters A single Juniper router feature generated a connection storm that grounded flights nationwide, a vivid lesson in change control and blast radius.

    Tom's Hardware, Transportation.gov, Cellstream · Nov 15, 2026 edition

  2. Routing·Analysis

    Invalid route to Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 propagated despite valid ROA

    On November 13–14 UTC, a route leak/hijack of Cloudflare's 1.1.1.0/24 DNS resolver prefix degraded Canadian resolver reachability.

    Why it matters A hijacked Cloudflare 1.1.1.0/24 route stayed RPKI Invalid yet still propagated, showing origin validation alone won't protect your resolvers.

    LogicMonitor · Nov 17, 2026 edition

  3. Telco·Industry news

    Telcos are writing their own network autonomy code

    Finland's Elisa trained its engineers to write the code that runs its Level 4 network. Lumen, Vodafone and Orange are also building their own network automation, with carriers setting the rules for what agents can do and when a human needs to step in.

    Why it matters Operators are hiring and training engineers to write their own autonomy code rather than buying it, reshaping NetDevOps career and tooling paths.

    Fierce Network · Nov 20, 2026 edition

  4. Startups·Industry news

    Traversal raises $120 million for autonomous AI site reliability engineers

    Traversal, which builds autonomous AI site reliability engineers to diagnose and resolve software outages, raised $120 million in Series B funding from Kleiner Perkins, Redpoint Ventures, Sequoia Capital, Battery Ventures, and Khosla Ventures at a $1 billion valuation.

    Why it matters A $120M Series B at $1B for autonomous AI SREs sets expectations and competition for anyone building incident response in-house.

    Parsers Substack / Tech Funding Digest · Nov 16, 2026 edition

  5. Automation·Industry news

    AutoCon 6 talks cover Nautobot federation and disposable labs

    AutoCon 6 (Nov 16–20, Tucson) features practitioner talks on federated Nautobot deployments, spreadsheet-to-SoT migration, disposable labs, mock-device testing, resumable upgrades, and agentic networking tools.

    Why it matters AutoCon 6 practitioner talks on federated Nautobot, disposable labs and mock-device testing are directly reusable patterns for production automation teams.

    Network Automation Forum · Nov 19, 2026 edition
