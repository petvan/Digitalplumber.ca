    Kentik adds agentic AI that investigates network incidents itself

    Kentik launched AI Advisor on November 18 as an agentic AI system that formulates network investigation plans, executes queries across telemetry sources, and presents findings with supporting evidence to automate root cause analysis.

    Why it matters Agentic RCA is moving into mainstream network observability; Kentik's AI Advisor runs its own investigation plans across telemetry, changing NOC triage workflows.

    Network World · Nov 24, 2026 edition

  2. Routing·Analysis

    Latent July gateway defect caused 24-hour Microsoft 365 outage

    A major global Microsoft outage spanning November 12-14, 2026 lasted 23 hours 50 minutes, affecting Exchange Online, Teams, and downstream services.

    Why it matters A 24-hour Microsoft 365 outage traced to a latent July gateway defect, failover load and 14% missing capacity is a reusable postmortem lesson.

    Office365itpros · Nov 18, 2026 edition

  3. Routing·Research

    BGP anomaly detectors miss up to 41% at unfamiliar collectors

    RIPE NCC researchers built a BGP anomaly detector using public Routing Information Service data and tested it blind on a network for three days, raising four alerts within an hour on the second morning that correctly matched actual incidents.

    Why it matters Blind-tested BGP anomaly detection worked, but detectors missed 35-41% of anomalies on other collectors, a warning about overfitting ML to one vantage point.

    RIPE Labs · Nov 23, 2026 edition

  4. Automation·Primary source

    SCinet builds the world's fastest network on Nautobot

    SCinet, the temporary supercomputing network at SC26, uses Nautobot as its network source of truth to automate design, deployment, and teardown of a large-scale research network.

    Why it matters SCinet's Nautobot-driven source of truth shows GitOps and automated cabling, IP and teardown workflows at extreme scale, a template for DevNetOps teams.

    Network to Code · Nov 21, 2026 edition

  5. DC networking·Industry news

    Lambda runs co-packaged optics across 10,000 GPUs in production

    Lambda has deployed Nvidia's co-packaged optics technology in an AI cluster connecting more than 10,000 Blackwell Ultra GPUs, demonstrating production-scale CPO viability.

    Why it matters First production co-packaged optics at 10,000+ GPUs changes power, reliability and serviceability assumptions for anyone designing AI fabrics.

    TechTarget · Nov 21, 2026 edition
