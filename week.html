    Selector introduced Selector Foundry, a development and runtime environment enabling network operations teams to build, test, version and govern their own AI agents where telemetry is correlated and stored in Git.

  3. Infrastructure·Vendor release

    WhiteFiber links data centres 83km apart at 136 Tbps

    WhiteFiber announced commercial availability of WhiteFiber Continuum, a cross-data-center networking solution that links geographically separated AI data centers into a single GPU supercluster.

    Why it matters Cross-DC GPU superclusters at 0.9ms over 83km redefine what counts as a single training fabric.

    Investing.com · Sep 26, 2026 edition

  4. Agentic AI·Vendor release

    NetBrain adds governed change and assessment agents

    NetBrain announced a major expansion of the NetBrain Agentic NetOps Platform, adding new Change and Assessment agents governed by an expanded NetOps Harness that enables AI agents and network engineers to work together across troubleshooting, change, assessment and prevention.

    Why it matters Governed change and assessment agents under one harness show how vendors are handling approval gates in production networks.

    NetBrain / Business Wire · Sep 22, 2026 edition

  5. Infrastructure·Research

    Marvell shows 2nm 1.6T coherent optics at ECOC

    Marvell demonstrated 2nm 800G ZR/ZR+ with MACsec and first 2nm 1.6T ZR and coherent-lite O-band optical technologies at ECOC 2026, powered by next-generation DSPs optimized for high-bandwidth connectivity within and between data center facilities and paving the way for 3.2T connectivity.

    Why it matters 2nm 1.6T coherent and co-packaged optics set the roadmap for interconnect once copper runs out inside AI pods.

    Marvell Technology · Sep 23, 2026 edition
