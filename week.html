  2. Security·Primary source

    Browsers revoke trust in eMudhra CAs over weak keys

    Eight intermediate CAs operated by eMudhra had their private keys compromised with catastrophically weak entropy—as few as 3 bits instead of the required 384 bits for NIST P-384.

    Why it matters Eight eMudhra intermediate CAs with 3-bit entropy keys mean imminent browser distrust dates and certificate replacement work for operators.

    eMudhra official statement; Google, Apple, Mozilla browser root program updates · Nov 8, 2026 edition

  3. Security·Industry news

    Three chained Ivanti zero-days give attackers root on VPNs

    Threat actor UTA0576 exploits three chained Ivanti Connect Secure flaws to achieve unauthenticated root-level remote code execution: CVE-2026-104935 (authentication bypass + RCE), CVE-2026-111357 (privilege escalation, CVSS 8.8), and CVE-2026-118527 (file upload).

    Why it matters Three chained Ivanti Connect Secure zero-days give unauthenticated root on VPN appliances, actively exploited since October and now in CISA KEV.

    The Hacker News · Nov 2, 2026 edition

  4. Telco·Primary source

    Verizon rolls out Ericsson AI controller across its network

    Verizon and Ericsson announced nationwide rollout of the Ericsson Intelligent Controller supporting AI-native rApps deployment. Field trials showed up to 8.9% improvement in downlink throughput and 8% in uplink throughput.

    Why it matters Verizon's nationwide Ericsson Intelligent Controller rollout is the largest concrete proof that AI-native RAN automation delivers measurable throughput gains.

    Ericsson · Nov 4, 2026 edition

  5. Research·Primary source

    OTel-Arrow phase 2 cuts telemetry pipeline network overhead

    OTel-Arrow Phase 2 extends OTAP transport protocol with the OTel-Arrow Dataflow Engine, a Rust runtime using OTAP as primary in-pipeline representation. Demonstrates significantly lower network overhead while preserving OpenTelemetry data model compatibility.

    Why it matters OTel-Arrow's Rust dataflow engine cuts telemetry network overhead sharply, directly affecting cost and design of large observability pipelines.

    OpenTelemetry Blog · Nov 8, 2026 edition
