    Broadcom ships Tomahawk 6 silicon with 1.6T Ethernet ports

    Broadcom announced production volume for Tomahawk 6 family switch silicon with 102.4 Tbps capacity and support for 1.6T Ethernet ports.

    Why it matters 102.4 Tbps Tomahawk 6 in volume, including co-packaged optics, resets AI fabric design assumptions for radix, cabling and power.

    Vultr orders $1.2B of HPE racks using Ethernet scale-up

    Vultr ordered HPE systems with 72 AMD Instinct MI455X GPUs per rack connected by six HPE Juniper Networking QFX5252 scale-up Ethernet switch trays using standards-based UALink over Ethernet (UALoE).

    Why it matters First big named win for UALink over Ethernet scale-up fabrics, signalling Ethernet encroaching on proprietary GPU interconnects.

    Converge Digest · Sep 30, 2026 edition

  4. Agentic AI·Industry news

    Most network operations leaders expect AI-led model within a year

    Omdia study of 1,000 IT and network operations leaders shows 84% expect AI-led operating model within 12 months, with 69% demanding detailed causal tracing for agentic AI actions and 86% wanting single integrated platforms over point tools.

    Why it matters Operators want causal tracing and single platforms for agentic NetOps, shaping tooling and observability requirements practitioners must now meet.

    ISE Magazine · Sep 30, 2026 edition

  5. AIOps·Primary source

    Forward Networks releases digital twin change validation generally

    Forward Networks announced general availability of Forward Predict, a capability that validates proposed network changes against a mathematically accurate digital twin before production deployment.

    Why it matters Pre-change validation against a digital twin is the control agentic network automation needs before autonomous changes touch production.

    Forward Networks press release · Oct 1, 2026 edition
