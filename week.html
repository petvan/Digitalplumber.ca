    NVIDIA and Grafana make NetBox their infrastructure source of truth

    NetBox becomes native to NVIDIA DSX OS and Grafana catalog. NVIDIA integrates NetBox as a source of truth for NVIDIA Configuration Manager (NVCM) for AI infrastructure orchestration.

    Why it matters NetBox becoming a source of truth inside NVIDIA's AI infrastructure orchestration and Grafana dashboards reshapes how automation teams wire up source of truth.

  2. AIOps·Vendor release

    Selector lets network teams build and version their own agents

    Selector introduced Selector Foundry, a development and runtime environment that lets network operations teams build, test, version and govern their own AI agents inside the Selector platform, where operators author agents where their telemetry is correlated, store them in Git, and validate against historical incidents before production.

    Why it matters Git-versioned, testable AI agents validated against historical incidents is the first credible DevNetOps workflow for agentic network operations.

    Selector · Oct 15, 2026 edition

  3. Security·Industry news

    Ivanti Connect Secure zero-day chain gives attackers root remotely

    CISA urged immediate patching of CVE-2026-104935, an Ivanti Connect Secure authentication bypass exploited in zero-day attacks since October 1st that lets remote threat actors execute arbitrary commands without credentials.

    Why it matters Unauthenticated root RCE chain actively exploited on Ivanti Connect Secure since October 1 demands immediate emergency patching of remote-access edge.

    BleepingComputer · Oct 14, 2026 edition

  4. DC networking·Industry news

    Ultra Ethernet 1.1 adds programmable congestion control for huge clusters

    UEC released Version 1.1 on October 13 to boost scale-up networking and improve congestion control as AI workloads surge through high-end networks.

    Why it matters UEC 1.1's programmable congestion management sets the Ethernet baseline for million-GPU fabrics engineers will be designing and tuning next.

    SDxCentral · Oct 15, 2026 edition

  5. Agentic AI·Industry news

    DE-CIX releases open-source MCP server for network provisioning

    DE-CIX released an open-source Model Context Protocol (MCP) server enabling network engineers to discover, procure, deploy and manage network connections through natural-language AI agents, with read-only and deployment capabilities.

    Why it matters An open-source MCP server from an exchange operator lets engineers provision and manage interconnects via natural language agents today.

    TechAfrica News · Oct 15, 2026 edition
