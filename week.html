  1. Agentic AI·Industry Trend

    Half of large enterprises run agentic AI in production networks

    Cisco and Omdia's study of 1,000 IT and network operations leaders shows 75% of organizations have already deployed AI for network operations, with more than half running agentic AI systems in production today and 84% expecting an AI-led operating model within 12 months. Over three-quarters are willing to grant agentic AI significant autonomy in NetOps, with 86% saying a single integrated platform is most effective.

    Why it mattersLarge-sample data shows agentic AI already making autonomous production network changes, setting expectations NetOps leaders will be measured against.

    Cisco Blogs / PR Newswire · Sep 23, 2026 edition

  2. Agentic AI·Product Launch

    NetBrain adds governed change and assessment agents to NetOps platform

    NetBrain announced a major expansion of the NetBrain Agentic NetOps Platform, adding new Change and Assessment agents governed by an expanded NetOps Harness that enables AI agents and network engineers to work together across troubleshooting, change, assessment and prevention. These new agents join the previously released Diagnosis agent, running on a common governance framework.

    Why it mattersNetBrain's governed change and assessment agents show what production-grade agentic NetOps tooling with guardrails actually looks like.

    NetBrain / Business Wire · Sep 22, 2026 edition

  3. Agentic AI·Product Launch

    Selector Foundry validates network agents against past incidents

    Selector introduced Selector Foundry, a development and runtime environment enabling network operations teams to build, test, version and govern their own AI agents where telemetry is correlated and stored in Git. Before promotion to production, agents are replayed against historical incident data for pass/fail validation against actual past outcomes.

    Why it mattersReplaying agents against historical incidents for pass/fail validation is a concrete CI-style pattern network teams can copy.

    Selector / Business Wire · Sep 22, 2026 edition

  4. Security·Industry Trend

    Cisco Talos finds first fully autonomous AI malware

    Cisco Talos disclosed CLOSEDQUORUM on September 22, 2026, described as the first reported fully autonomous multi-model AI command-and-control implant that operates with no human operator, and released CAIRN, a toolkit for hunting AI-integrated malware. Such malware has moved from optional AI helpers to autonomous command and control within about a year.

    Why it mattersFirst fully autonomous AI command-and-control implant changes threat models and detection tooling for network defenders.

    AI News Today (buildfastwithai.com) · Sep 25, 2026 edition

  5. Infrastructure·Industry Trend

    Networking becomes the choke point in AI data centres

    The real bottleneck in AI training clusters is not silicon but the fiber, switches, and optics surrounding it—data cannot move fast enough between chips when the network is constrained. Hyperscalers poured capex into network fabric, with Cisco booking $9.3 billion of AI infrastructure orders in fiscal 2026 while calling the moment a 'networking supercycle.' Arista raised its 2026 revenue outlook to roughly $12.6 billion (40% annual growth), with its AI Fabrics segment on track for at least $3.5 billion in 2026.

    Why it mattersNetwork fabric, not GPUs, is the AI cluster bottleneck, and vendor order books confirm where budget is going.

    24/7 Wall Street · Sep 25, 2026 edition

  6. Networking

    Juniper Apstra Freeform automates any data centre topology

    Juniper Networks expanded its Apstra intent-based networking platform with Freeform capability, enabling enterprises to manage and automate data center operations regardless of topology and protocols. This removes architectural constraints from intent-driven networking, allowing customers to move beyond rigid fabric designs to flexible, multi-topology environments across data centers.

    Why it mattersApstra Freeform removes topology constraints from intent-based automation, widening multivendor data center automation options.

    Digitalisation World · Sep 19, 2026 edition

  7. Infrastructure·Research

    Marvell demonstrates 2nm 1.6T coherent optics at ECOC

    Marvell demonstrated 2nm 800G ZR/ZR+ with MACsec and first 2nm 1.6T ZR and coherent-lite O-band optical technologies at ECOC 2026, powered by next-generation DSPs optimized for high-bandwidth connectivity within and between data center facilities and paving the way for 3.2T connectivity. Advanced co-packaged optics (CPO) technology integrates optics directly with AI accelerators and switch silicon as per-lane speeds and multi-rack AI pods push past copper's reach limits.

    Why it matters2nm 1.6T coherent optics and co-packaged optics define the interconnect roadmap for multi-rack AI pods.

    Marvell Technology · Sep 23, 2026 edition

  8. Telco

    Nokia and Microsoft cut telco data prep to minutes

    Telecom engineers pairing Nokia Data Suite with Microsoft Fabric can deploy agentic network automation across infrastructure in minutes, delivering a unified data foundation that unites structured network telemetry with cloud-scale analytics. The pairing cuts the time operators need to reach usable network data from multiple weeks down to minutes. Initial use cases include VoNR assurance, RAN optimization and predictive maintenance, with the solution available now.

    Why it mattersNokia-Microsoft pairing cuts telco network data preparation from weeks to minutes, enabling practical RAN and assurance automation.

    Telecom's Technews · Sep 19, 2026 edition
